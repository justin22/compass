# Compass privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Compass does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any of your data.

**What it reads.** To work, Compass reads your open tabs, bookmarks and browsing history. It uses them only to show and search them in the sidebar and command bar.

**What it stores.** Archived tabs, collapsed folder state and the IDs of its two bookmark folders are kept in your browser's local extension storage. For the tab switcher's previews, Compass keeps a small screenshot of each tab you have looked at. These stay in memory only: one is removed when its tab closes, and all of them are gone when the browser closes. Favorites and pinned tabs are saved as normal bookmarks, so they sync through your browser account the same way your other bookmarks do.

**What it sends.** Nothing. Compass makes no network requests, has no analytics or tracking, and loads no remote code. When you search the web from the command bar, the search goes to your browser's default search engine, the same as typing in the address bar.

**Removing data.** Uninstalling Compass deletes its local storage. The "Favorites" and "Pinned" bookmark folders stay until you delete them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/justin22/compass/issues
